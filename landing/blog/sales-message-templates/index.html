30 Sales Message Templates for WhatsApp and Text (Copy and Paste)

Good sales messages are short, specific and end with a clear next step. Below are 30 templates organized by the moment of the sale. Copy the ones that fit your business, change the details in brackets and save them so you never write them from scratch again.

A template is not a script you read without thinking. It's a starting point that saves you from a blank screen when a customer is waiting. Always read the customer's message first, then adjust the template so it answers exactly what they asked.

Words in [brackets] are placeholders. Replace them with your own details.

First contact

1 · Quick acknowledgement

Hi [name]! Thanks for your message. I'm getting the details on [product] for you right now.

2 · Inbound from an ad

Hi [name], thanks for your interest in [product]! Here are the photos and the price. Is it for you or a gift?

3 · Qualifying question

Happy to help! So I can send the best options: what's your budget range, and when do you need it?

4 · Referral

Hi [name], [referrer] told me you're looking for [product/service]. I'd love to help. What matters most to you in [category]?

Product and price

5 · Product card

[Product name]
Price: [price]
[Key detail 1] · [Key detail 2]
Available in: [colors/sizes/dates]
Want me to reserve one for you?

6 · Price with context

It's [price], and that includes [what's included]. Most customers choose it because [main benefit].

7 · Two options

I have two options that fit what you described: [option A] for [price A] and [option B] for [price B]. The main difference is [difference]. Which one sounds closer?

8 · Out of stock

That one is sold out right now, but [alternative] is very similar and available today. Want to see photos? I can also let you know when [original] is back.

9 · Custom quote

Thanks for the details! I'll put together a quote and send it by [time]. Is this the best number to reach you?

Handling doubts and objections

10 · “It's expensive”

I understand. The price reflects [quality/warranty/service]. If it helps, [payment plan / smaller option] could work for you. Want me to send it?

11 · “I'll think about it”

Of course, take your time. Is there anything I can clarify to make the decision easier?

12 · “I found it cheaper”

Thanks for telling me. Could you check if that offer includes [warranty/delivery/support]? If it does, let me see what I can do.

13 · “Is it worth it?”

Here's what our customers usually say after a month: [short testimonial]. And if [condition], you can [return/exchange] within [period].

14 · Needs approval from someone else

Makes sense! Want me to send a short summary with photos and price that you can forward to [them]?

Scheduling and visits

15 · Offer two times

I can show it to you [day] at [time 1] or [time 2]. Which works better?

16 · Confirmation

You're all set for [day] at [time] at [address]. I'll send you a reminder the day before. See you there!

17 · Reminder

Hi [name], just a reminder about our appointment tomorrow at [time]. Still good for you?

18 · Reschedule

No problem at all! What day works better for you this week?

Closing and payment

19 · Ready to buy

Great choice! Here's the payment link: [link]. As soon as it's confirmed, I'll [ship/schedule] it.

20 · Payment methods

We accept [methods]. Which one is easiest for you?

21 · Payment received

Payment received, thank you! Your order number is [number]. I'll let you know as soon as it's on the way.

22 · Reservation with a deadline

I'll hold it for you until [day/time]. After that it goes back on sale, okay?

Delivery and after-sale

23 · Shipped

Your order is on the way! Tracking: [link]. Expected delivery: [date].

24 · Delivered, check-in

Hi [name]! Did everything arrive okay? Let me know if you need anything.

25 · Returns and exchanges

You can exchange it within [period] as long as [condition]. Just reply here and I'll take care of it.

26 · Review request

So glad you liked it! If you have a minute, a quick review here would help us a lot: [link]. Thank you!

Follow-ups

27 · No reply after a quote

Hi [name], did you get a chance to look at the quote? Happy to adjust anything.

28 · Something new

Hi [name], [product] you asked about is back in stock / has a new price: [detail]. Want me to save one for you?

29 · Repeat customer

Hi [name]! It's been a while since your [last purchase]. We just got [new product] and thought of you. Want to see it?

30 · After hours

Thanks for your message! We're available [days] from [hours]. I'll reply first thing [tomorrow/Monday]. Feel free to send any details in the meantime.

How to make templates sound like you

  • Use the customer's name and the exact product they mentioned. It's the fastest way to make a saved reply feel personal.
  • Keep your own voice. If you'd never say “greetings”, don't write it. Read the template out loud; if it doesn't sound like you, change it.
  • One question per message. Customers answer easy questions quickly and ignore long lists.
  • Review them every few months. Prices, hours and policies change. An outdated template costs more trust than it saves time.
