What you type stays on your phone

A keyboard sees everything you type. Here is exactly what leaves your phone, and what never does.

Stays on your phone

  • Everything you type. It is processed on the phone to correct words and suggest the next ones, and is never stored or sent.
  • Your products, shortcuts, customers, calendar and file folders.
  • Translations, which run on the phone itself.

Goes to our servers

  • Your account and profile: email, name, business name, WhatsApp and industry.
  • Photos, videos and audio you upload, saved as public links so they can be sent in chats.

On iPhone, why Full Access? iOS asks every keyboard that uses the internet to allow Full Access, and warns that it could send what you type. KEYCON needs it to read your catalog from the app and to copy photos for you to paste. Your typing is still processed only on the phone.

Common questions

Does it work on WhatsApp?

Yes. KEYCON is a keyboard, so it works in any app where you type: WhatsApp, Instagram, Messenger, e-mail and more.

Does it work on Android and iPhone?

Yes. On iPhone, due to Apple’s rules, photos and videos are copied by the keyboard and you paste them into the chat with one tap.

Do I have to replace my phone keyboard?

KEYCON can be your everyday keyboard, with spell check and suggestions. You can switch back to the system keyboard at any time with the globe button in the menu.

Does KEYCON read what I type?

No. What you type is processed only on the phone, to correct and suggest words, and is never sent to our servers.

How much does it cost?

KEYCON will be a monthly subscription. Prices will be announced at launch.

Which languages does it support?

The app and the keyboard are in English, Portuguese and Spanish. The keyboard translator also covers French, Italian and German.
